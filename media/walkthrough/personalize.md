# Settings worth knowing

**Isabelle: Markup Colors**: `theme` colours checked text from your colour theme
(try a few); `isabelle` uses Isabelle/jEdit's palette.

**Isabelle: Check Whole Theory**: check every open theory to its end rather than
50 lines past the cursor. Toggle it any time:
[Toggle Checking the Whole Theory](command:isabelle.toggleWholeTheoryChecking)

**Isabelle: Render Symbols** and **Reveal Symbol At Cursor**: draw glyphs over the
ASCII, and show the escape under the cursor.

**Isabelle: Input: Custom Shorthands**: your own `\` shortcuts, for example
`{"cup": "\<union>"}`.

**Isabelle: Auto Start**: turn off to read theories without starting a prover.
